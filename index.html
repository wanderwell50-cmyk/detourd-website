Enter your route.

Platform Features

Built for how people actually travel.

🧭
Route Intelligence
Automatically surfaces stops within a customizable corridor around your route - from 5 to 50 miles wide - without breaking your travel time budget.
⭐
Crowd-Sourced Ratings
Aggregates verified reviews from TripAdvisor, Yelp, Google, and AllTrails so you always know which stops are worth the detour and which aren't.
🏛️
National Parks & Landmarks
Every National Park, state park, UNESCO site, and iconic landmark near your route surfaces automatically - with entry fees, hours, timed-entry permit links, and guided tour options via Viator and GetYourGuide.
🏕️
Scenic & Outdoor Stops
Highlights trail access points, wildflower meadows, summit overlooks, and campgrounds - so the miles between parks are just as rewarding as the parks themselves.
🍜
Local Haunt Finder
Skip the chains. Detourd surfaces independently owned restaurants, food trucks, and farm stands that locals actually love - not just what's sponsored.
💆
Wellness Along the Way
Surfacing top-rated spas, hot springs, yoga studios, and wellness centers for travelers who want to recharge mid-journey, not just at the destination.
📅
Real-Time Availability
Live booking data from Booking.com, OpenTable, and partner APIs means every stop you see can be reserved in one tap, with live pricing and open slots.
🔔
Drive-Time Alerts
Approaching a highly-rated stop? Detourd pings you with a brief window to decide - so you never miss a legendary roadside pie shop again.
✈️
Flight Search via Skyscanner
Flying to your starting point or home from your destination? Search and compare flights directly through Detourd - powered by Skyscanner's live pricing across hundreds of airlines.
🗂️
Trip Collections
Save, share, and revisit full trip itineraries with friends. Build collections by theme - "California Wine Runs," "Rocky Mountain Loops" - and share them publicly.
🏆
Ranked by Travelers, Not Dollars
Every stop is ranked purely by verified user ratings. No business can pay to appear higher in your results - ever. A family diner with 800 five-star reviews always beats a chain with an ad budget.
🤝
Seamless Booking Handoffs
Every recommendation connects to a trusted booking platform via affiliate links - one tap to reserve, with no dead ends or extra steps between you and the stop.
